# Readshot v0.9.0

## Summary

- **A new look.** Every window has been redesigned. The app follows the
  system light or dark appearance, uses one set of icons everywhere, and
  has floating tool and action bars, toasts for status messages, and a
  sharper 16 px menu-bar icon.
- **Capture Window…** in the menu bar lights up the window under the
  pointer and captures the one you click. You can give it a shortcut in
  Settings › Capture.
- **More editing tools:** Spotlight (S), Stamps with symbols and emoji
  (E), a Magnifier callout (M), an eyedropper (I), custom colours with a
  hex field and a Recent row, and filled, dashed and rounded shapes,
  curved and hand-drawn arrows, and pill or bold text. Each tool has an
  options bar, and ⌥⌘0 fits the image to the window.
- **Image menu:** resize, rotate and flip a capture, crop with presets
  (16:9, 4:3, square) and thirds guides, or combine it with another
  capture or an image file.
- **Text panel:** copy recognised lines one by one, see them outlined on
  the image, and redact email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers and
  API keys with one click (solid block, pixelate or blur). History
  copies of a capture are cleaned when you redact it. The highlighter
  snaps to lines of text.
- **Beautify** replaces Frame style: background, padding, rounded
  corners, shadow and canvas size, with up to 12 presets of your own.
- **Settings and History:** Settings has a sidebar with seven panes
  (⌘1–⌘7). History has title-bar search with highlighted matches.
  Scrolling capture shows a live stitched preview and stops on its own
  before it gets too large. Pins have no frame and show their controls
  on hover.
- **Keyboard and accessibility:** keyboard paths for windows that used
  to need the mouse, visible focus rings, and stronger contrast. Held
  undo, redo and step keys repeat again.
- **Reliability:** large images are composed off the main thread, so the
  editor stays responsive. History survives crashes and unreadable
  preferences files. The CLI refuses to uninstall while the app is
  running. And hundreds of smaller fixes.
